# M01-I3B-2H — Global activity UX hardening

Date: 2026-10-11 · Windows 11 · Node 24.14 · Electron 44.7.0 (Chromium 152.0.7977.130) · branch `feature/m01-i3b-2h-global-activity`, based on the last commit of M01-I3B-2 (418 automated tests; manual homologation pending) · Herdr 0.9.3.

Problem: the I3B-2 indicators live on the conversation strip, so the user has to be on the ChatGPT tab and in the right workspace to learn that a conversation elsewhere is responding or needs review, while the point of ContextDeck is to keep working in Herdr or another project. Scope: (1) a permanent global indicator, (2) an activity center for every workspace with direct navigation to a conversation, (3) discreet in-app notices. **Not** done, by instruction: Windows notifications, sounds, history, unloading, new detectors, any change to ChatGPT's behaviour, I3C. Decision record: [0003](../decisions/0003-multi-view-navigation.md) (second amendment); architecture: [architecture.md](../architecture.md#global-activity-m01-i3b-2h).

Legend: **Auto** = automated suite · **Harness** = `test/electron/activity-harness.js`, real Electron (real window, views, hub, navigator, overlay, IPC and renderer), throwaway profile, logged-out chatgpt.com, fake Herdr, **no message ever sent** (I3P synthetic signals on the page's own composer) · **Manual (pending)** = needs the project owner, logged in, real Herdr and real responses · **Blocked**.

## A. Initial diagnosis

| # | Topic | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Information already global | The hub holds a summary per loaded view for every workspace; `getWorkspaceActivity()` already aggregates counts per workspace; the Herdr state push already carries them to the selector | No new observation: the global state is a projection of the hub's summaries |
| 2 | What was missing | Per-conversation detail across workspaces (labels, states, openability), totals, and an *event* for "new pending" | `navigator.getGlobalActivity()`, `resolveActivityTarget()`, hub `onAttention` |
| 3 | Where state reaches the renderer | `chatnav:state` (strip, derived per workspace), `herdr:state` (selector), `chats:state` (indicator/panel); the overlay gets only its own pushes | A small `activity:state` payload (UI window + overlay) with a pusher that sends only changed content |
| 4 | Overlay | One transparent view, modal modes (menu, bindings panel), idle-released after 30 s, covers the content area with a scrim | Two more modes: activity center (modal) and notice cards (the SAME view as a small rectangle, never focused, so no scrim and no input capture) |
| 5 | Native views | The ChatGPT view paints over the local DOM; z-index cannot overlay it | Center and notices live in the overlay view (added last, re-raised when another view is attached); the indicator is in the top bar, which no native view covers |
| 6 | Selection | `selection.select()` = the explicit user selection (`workspace.focus`, validated, honouring Auto/Manual, single-flight, confirms against a fresh snapshot) | Direct navigation reuses it; it is the only Herdr write reachable |
| 7 | Attention policy | Cleared only by visits (explicit actions that show the view, window/tab interactivity, typing) | Opening from the center goes through `navigator.selectBinding()` — the same visit, no second rule |
| 8 | Focus | `overlay.show()` focuses the overlay; the window `focus` handler skips restoring focus while `isOpen()` | `isOpen()` stays "a modal"; notices use `showNotice()` which never focuses |

### Semantics

| Indicator | Meaning | Not meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `↻ n` (spinner ring) | n loaded, observed conversations are **responding** (the stop control is observed) | that they will finish |
| `● m` | m conversations have **activity to review**: an observed response stopped being observed while the user could not see the view, and nobody visited it since | that the response completed, was stopped or failed |
| `✎ k` | k conversations have unsent text in the composer (presence only) | anything about the text |
| `○ Atividade` | no observed activity | that the app is idle: "N observadas de M carregadas" is in the tooltip; conversations never loaded are not observed and never counted |

### Attention rules (unchanged from I3B-2, now reachable from everywhere)

Raised by the model, cleared only by a **visit**. Not cleared by opening the center, selecting a workspace, hovering, a notice disappearing or being dismissed, selecting another conversation, or the view staying hidden. Opening a conversation from the center or a notice is an explicit selection of the tab: the navigator performs the visit if (and only if) the view is then user-visible (chat tab, window interactive). A visit is not proof of reading.

### Direct navigation (security between workspaces)

1. Resolve against current state: the binding must belong to that workspace in this session; orphans and other sessions → `not_available`; suspect/unverified → `confirmation_required` **before any command** (the UI asks for a second click).
2. If another workspace is selected and no switch is in flight: `selection.select()` (explicit user action; Auto/Manual rules of M01-I2 apply). Failure → stop: the chat tab is **not** shown, no conversation is selected, the message says the conversation was not opened (and that the workspace did not change).
3. The latest request wins (a refused one does not invalidate the one being served); after the switch the selected workspace must equal the target, otherwise `context_changed` and nothing is shown (Auto may have followed another focus; the message says whether Herdr did move).
4. Show the chat tab (a user-level switch), then `navigator.selectBinding()`: reuses the loaded view, no reload, no destroy, no binding created or changed.
Two systems are involved; atomicity is not simulated: a failure after the switch is reported as partial ("O workspace foi trocado, mas a conversa nao foi aberta"). Status updates themselves never send `workspace.focus`.

### Notices

Raised on a new pending attention only; not for reload, timeout, detector loss, internal navigation, unknown state, startup/restore (the first summary is a baseline) or never-observed conversations. One card per view (a repeat refreshes it), newest first, ≤ 3 shown + "+N avisos", expire after 8 s, retired when the attention is acknowledged, shown only while the window is interactive and no modal owns the overlay (they stay in the center). They take no focus, no scrim, no animation. Reopening the center does not re-raise them.

## E. Risks and limitations

1. **Detection is polling**: a hidden view's response is noticed within 5 s, its end within 1 s while it responds; a very short response can be missed (no indicator, by design).
2. **"Atividade para revisar" ≠ "concluída"**: the detector cannot distinguish completion, interruption and failure; the wording and the notices say so.
3. **Windows notices are not implemented**: if the window is minimized or unfocused the card is not shown (the indicator and the center have the information when you return; a card that expired meanwhile is gone, the pending state is not).
4. **Overlay cost**: the notice cards keep the overlay renderer alive while shown (≈ 100 MB), released after 30 s idle; there is no extra persistent view.
5. **Notices vs modals**: while a menu/panel/center is open the cards are hidden (they stay in the center). A modal opened by mistake never hides the indicator.
6. **Direct open and Auto mode**: Auto may follow another Herdr focus right after the switch; the flow then reports `context_changed` and shows nothing under the wrong workspace.
7. **Two sources of time**: the real Herdr round trip is not part of the harness numbers.
8. The indicator counts only loaded, observed conversations; temporary conversations are listed under their workspace and can be opened through the same flow; the unscoped page cannot be opened from the center.

## F. Pending for I3C and later
